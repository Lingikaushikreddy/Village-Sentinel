# Village strategy game

Play at https://lingikaushikreddy.github.io/Village-Sentinel/.

The single-player browser simulation lets you place farms, timber camps, trading posts and watchtowers on an isometric grid. Buildings consume coins and timber. Farms generate food, camps produce timber and trading posts earn coins every five game seconds. Villagers consume food every ten seconds. Watchtowers provide village-wide defense against a raid every thirty seconds, with each raid stronger than the last. Upgrade buildings through level three; trade sixteen coins for eight food, or spend ten coins and twenty timber to repair twenty-five integrity.

Choose a building in the construction bar, then click a highlighted tile. Roofs become translucent during placement. Click a structure to upgrade it, or use “Manage a building” when another roof obscures it. On phones, swipe the map horizontally. Pause freezes village time; 2× advances twice as fast. Hidden pages do not advance. Progress saves on this device, with no offline production or cross-device account. “New village” asks before replacing that save.

Win by constructing eight buildings, repelling three raids and finishing at least one AI mission. Zero integrity ends the village. A completed AI mission grants forty coins and twenty-five timber once per scenario per village. Restarting that scenario does not multiply its reward.

The builder is deterministic application code. Its placement, economy, raids and workers do not invoke a model. The existing AI missions have their own world, observations and evidence. The public site replays real saved Decider results with a labeled Bedrock stand-in. These results do not read a player's custom buildings. Locally configured live mode retains the existing real Decider path. AWS and live Bedrock remain pending account setup.

Painted terrain and building artwork are reused from the user’s MIT-licensed Settlement-Village reference. The six supplied characters appear as transparent sprites on the map and in the inspector. See ART-PROVENANCE.md and ATTRIBUTION.md for the prior-work boundary. The game contains no Brawl Stars or Clash of Clans assets or multiplayer service.

The six helpers have local village jobs: Mira farms, Pip scouts for timber, Bea gathers supplies, Gus builds, Luna trades, and Nimbus delivers goods. Their teaching roles in AI missions remain separate. Select a character on the map or crew rail to inspect their current job, waiting reason and delivery count. Give them a twenty-second rest to stop their current job. Drag empty ground, use the camera buttons, or focus the map and use arrow keys to explore; Home recenters it.

Village orders are deterministic game AI. Restock completes twelve deliveries. Grow builds one additional farm, timber camp and watchtower with a ceiling of 200 coins and 120 timber. Fortify upgrades an existing watchtower until total defense reaches 42 and repairs integrity, with a ceiling of 240 coins and 180 timber. Automatic payments preserve 20 coins and 20 timber. Crew deliveries add to passive production: 5 coins, 6 timber or 8 food per completed collection, capped at the village resource limit. Canceling an order cancels pending work without charging for it. Re-selecting an order starts a new order and spending allowance. If funds or routes are unavailable, the resident waits; a new order or manual building can resolve the situation. Saved jobs recheck routes when loaded.

Run `npm run test:strategy` with the game server running to exercise building, upgrades, trade, mission rewards, save/reload, invalid-save recovery, browser Back navigation, reset, pause/production, raid visuals, six-character jobs, resting, camera controls and mobile layout. `SENTINEL_TEST_URL` can point to the static preview or public site. The tests use isolated browser contexts and spend nothing.
