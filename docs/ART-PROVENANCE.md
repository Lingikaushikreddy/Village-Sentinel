- `web/public/assets/character-sprites-v2.png`: transparent adaptation of the user's supplied cast sheet, created October 16, 2026 through the built-in image generation tool (`transparent_background: true`), not the API/CLI fallback. The alpha channel was checked: transparent background pixels are zero; character interiors are present. The bitmap is copied into the repo unchanged. SVG viewports select individual characters without modifying the source bitmap.

## Final generation prompt

Use case: background-extraction. Edit target: the attached six-character cast sheet. Create a clean transparent-background sprite atlas for the Village Sentinel browser game. Preserve these exact six designs, faces, outfits, colors and props: top left curly-haired brown-skinned young farmer in lilac overalls holding wheat basket; top middle ivory and teal round robot holding three cards; top right golden owl with spectacles, rust red scarf, pencil and notebook; bottom left black and white badger in dark green coat with round wood shield and clipboard; bottom middle brown beaver in coral apron holding one coin and a ledger; bottom right friendly pale blue cloud with blue limbs holding a server. Retain the 3 columns by 2 rows ordering. Each full-body character centered within its own equal-sized cell, feet on consistent baselines, ample transparent padding on every side, no overlap between cells. Keep all six full characters visible including antenna, ears, shield, beaver tail and cloud hand. Remove the entire dark gradient backdrop and colored glows completely. Actual transparent alpha outside the characters, not a checkerboard graphic. Keep the polished hand-painted cartoon style, no text, no labels, no borders, no ground shadows, no new objects or characters. Wide landscape sprite atlas.
